## Notes

- Non-empty stacks are written into the policy account directly; installing through the registry is tested in `tests/hooks/tests/tokens.rs` and does not change evaluation cost.
- In the transaction rows, the hooked total minus the plain transfer minus `execute: empty stack` is Token-2022's own cost of resolving the extra-account-metas list and invoking the hook.
- The credential row includes reading one gateway-token account. The transaction rows omit credential, because its accounts come from the extra-account-metas list.
- Regression gate: `cargo test -p hook-bench` fails if any case exceeds `bench/baseline.json` by more than 5%.
